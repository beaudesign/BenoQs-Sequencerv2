**Intend to change:** the strip gets a Metronome button, and the worklet writes a click into its own audio output, one for each quarter note (24 MIDI clock pulses) while the transport runs, from the engine's tick position. No engine, ABI, contract, golden or threshold change.
**How I'll know it worked:** a click at the frame of MIDI clock pulse 24 k in a run that changes tempo three times (`test/metronome.test.ts`); silence with it off, while stopped, and after the tail; a Play after a Stop clicks the beat it had not reached; the events of a run are the same bytes with it on and off; and in Chromium the node's output is exactly silent off and carries a click on.

### What I learned and what surprised me

- **The worklet's `reset` message is not the engine's Reset.** `octoweb_reset` drops the whole engine (`HOST = None`), and every command after it fails with code 1. My first test sent it and expected "a new run"; the page never sends it. The test is gone and the plan says so; a position that goes back to 0 is pinned in the pure Metronome instead, which keeps no count.
- A first test that read `rig.take()` after `run()` saw nothing: `block()` already takes what it posted. Fixed in the test, not the rig.
- The click needed no state at all. `beatsIn(p0, p1)` is strict at the end, so the beats of one block and the next never share an edge; a Stop that lands exactly on a beat is clicked at the Play's first frame, not lost and not twice (tested at a block edge).
- `pkill -f` matched its own shell's command line and killed the shell. Not used again.
