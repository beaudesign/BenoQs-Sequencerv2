P4e: the first pulse of the S3 send run, late by more than 100 ms in some runs, and the fix (apps/web/spikes/s3/page.ts).
Found by the Chromium test "send, from the page's own buttons" failing 3 times in 6 (`late` 1, expected 0), after it had passed once. The page
started the transport right after the audio context. Chromium (141, headless, no sound device) starts a context with a jump in the frame
count (handoffs/evidence/p4d-startup-frame-jump.txt), which leaves a hole in the engine's timeline; a transport started in it has its first
pulse (audio time 0) stamped for a page time that has already passed. Per-pulse margins are now saved in the file (raw.sent[].marginMs).
Whether a real device does this is not measured; the app never plays that early (Start, then Play), so this is about this page, not the app.
