## What I learned

- The hairline tone `edge` (1.28:1) is right for a rule under the strip and wrong for dotted lines: they vanished in the rendered check. The dots are in `edge.control`; ADR-0011 decision 10 now says why.
- **Finding for the Referee (S15):** the `rx` and `ry` scan also catches an `<ellipse>` element's attributes, and an ellipse is not a rounded rectangle. I drew the ellipses as two arcs rather than loosen the rule; the rule could name `<rect>` and `rect`-only attributes instead. A request, not a change.

- `#stage` as a size container takes its size from the page and nothing from its children, which is also why the plate's aspect has to reach the stylesheet as a number (`--panel-aspect`).
