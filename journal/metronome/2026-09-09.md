### What I learned

Inactive steps *do* participate in Track Rotate — p.38 says "all steps" except
skip/hyperstep/mask. First tests assumed only active steps rotated and failed
because step 15's empty content landed on step 0. The unused tail has to be
skipped in fixtures, not filtered in the algorithm.
