- **"Accept p4"** was read as accept the plan with its defaults, and written down as plan section 10 with what it does *not* answer:
  D0, the S1 and S3 runs, and merging. The series is revised from "none stacked" to stacked slices, each saying "merge in order", as P3c did.
- **A smaller way found while writing the ADR, and flagged in it.** The plan's default for D-P4-3 was new `Event` variants. A search for the
  `ChannelPressure` arms found twelve exhaustive matches on `Event` in eight files, two of them in `octoffi` (frozen) and `octorun`, and
  `octoffi.h` mirrors the tags by hand. A side channel (`Realtime`, `take_realtime`) gives the same pulses at the same samples and touches none of
  them, and no golden hash. ADR-0009 decision 1 says so and the owner can reject it before P4c starts; the intent of D-P4-3 (the engine emits
  them) is kept. Likewise D-P4-12's four event kinds became one kind with the status byte in `d1`.
- **A gap in the plan, closed in the ADR.** D-P4-4 said "tempo and phase follow" without saying how the page sees the engine's phase, which falls inside
  a render block. The ADR adds a read accessor, `Engine::tick_position`, as its own item in the request so it can be declined without the clock.
- **I overclaimed once and fixed it before committing.** The first ADR draft said the engine's pulse and step "agree by construction whatever D0
  decides". They share a first sample and cannot drift apart, but whether a step is a whole number of pulses is exactly D0 (1.5 now, 6 if the tick is
  48 to the quarter note). The ADR now says that.
