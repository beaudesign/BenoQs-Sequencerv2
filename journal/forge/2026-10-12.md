## What I learned

- A rule can hold vacuously. `the_panel_borrows_nothing_from_the_strip` passed on the old stylesheet because no rule was scoped to the panel, so there was nothing for it to look at. `the_panels_parts_are_styled_only_under_the_panel` closes that: a part of the panel styled from outside `#panel` is refused, so the first rule can never be sidestepped by an unscoped one.
- The Chromium suite is load-sensitive in one place: a 3 s `waitForFunction` in the lookahead test timed out once while another job ran (in P8a), and passes alone and on a clean rerun. Reported in P8a; not changed.
- `flash.shine` names the strip's ink. On the plate that is invisible (about 1.1:1), so the panel's shine is a heavier rim in `plate.ink` and the token is unused there. Request to the Conductor filed.
