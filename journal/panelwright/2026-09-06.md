### What I did

Manual: split into per-page text, topic-indexed, wired into `just manual`. The
PDF's own page count and the manual's printed footer numbers turned out to be
offset by 2 — verified by extracting every page's own footer rather than assuming
a constant offset, since an appendix or divider page could easily have broken
that assumption partway through. It didn't (offset holds constant pp.1-112), but
the last 10 PDF pages turned out to be a separately-bundled 2007 tutorial-series
appendix with its own, different footer format — kept those as `tutorial-NN.txt`
rather than forcing them into the main numbering.

Photo: one frontal product shot, uncalibrated, no scale reference, no
ColorChecker, single angle. Added to `reference/plates/` with an honest NOTES
entry (see that file) — it's useful for a topology/count sanity check against
`docs/01-panel-truth.md` §4's expected inventory, and nothing more. Set up
git-lfs for `reference/plates/*` while I was here (SPEC.md §5 already calls for
it; better to wire it now, for one small file, than migrate later once more/larger
plates land).

### What I learned

The tutorial-series appendix (PDF pages 115-124) isn't just filler — "Editing
track directions" (tutorial pp.1-2) contains a full worked walkthrough of
multi-trigger direction slices that directly resolves a design question Metronome
had flagged as a guess (whether multiple triggers in one slice fire sequentially
within a single visit, or round-robin one-per-visit — it's the former: "since you
added 4 step triggers... that track will be 4 steps behind the rest of the
sequence" only makes sense if every extra trigger consumes real playback time
within the same visit). Worth remembering that appendix material can outrank a
plausible-sounding guess even when the main numbered chapters don't cover it.
