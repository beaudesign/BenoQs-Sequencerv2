## What I learned, and what surprised me

- **The manual does not say which matrix row shows VEL, PIT, LEN or STA** (findings section 6, item 6, hardware). The P5 plan's W1 said the knob's row would show the value; that could not be asserted, so the plan is corrected (r1) and the drawing is a pending fixture. The knob-to-value half is fully specified and asserted.
- p068 shows velocity in the numeric quadrant of the outer circle and pitch in the inner circle in EDIT preview, and p015 shows both in rows. Both at once or one per mode is not stated (Q42). It bears on P5e's display output.
- p068 also says that in EDIT preview, grabbing a step in Page view and turning an attribute knob plays the step again on every click. That is the Audition intent, not built (Q41).
- The engine has no legato (`LengthTicks` clamps to 1 to 192), though p016 says a decrement below 1/192 is legato (Q40). The engine does clamp STA to plus or minus 5 and LEN to 192, which are exactly p016's limits.
- **A mutation run that does not use `--no-fail-fast` misleads:** cargo stops at the first failing test binary, the unit tests run first, and the fixtures then look as if they caught nothing. My first run said so for K1, K2, K5, K6 and K7; the second run showed the fixtures catch them too.
