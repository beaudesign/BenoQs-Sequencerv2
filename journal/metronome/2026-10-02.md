**Intend to change:** give the engine an optional MIDI clock master and a read of where the audio is on the tick grid, additively, with `Event`, `Command`, the wire
format, `octoffi`, `octorun` and every golden hash untouched.
**How I'll know it worked:** `crates/octocore/tests/clock.rs` (red first) pins every pulse's sample at five tempo and rate pairs and five block sizes; with the clock off

- **Gate numbers:** `cargo test --workspace` green; `cargo xtask verify --base origin/main`: 4 pass, 0 fail, 6 not_implemented; assertions 503 (baseline 462).
- **Mutation run:** 16 deliberate breakages in the engine, 15 caught on the first run. E9 (a render that makes more messages than the engine keeps, not counted)

### What I learned and what surprised me

- **The ADR was wrong about the first note.** ADR-0009 decision 2 said the first pulse and the first step are one sample. They are not: the engine's first note
  after Play is on tick 11 (`AMBIGUITIES.md`), so a drum machine on this clock plays its first step 1.4 pulses after Start. The clock is right and the pattern's
  own offset is not the clock's to hide. Said in amendment 1 and pinned by name.
- **Play and Continue are one call.** The engine does not rewind on Stop, so it cannot tell which was meant; it decides Start or Continue by whether the sequencer is
  at tick 0. That is the only honest choice, and the ADR had assumed two commands.
- **`tick_position` could not be `global_tick`.** The engine runs up to 12 ticks ahead of the audio, so the count is not where the speakers are. A 32-entry ring of
  due samples gives the audio's place, and a test that renders 125 samples at a time and expects a whole number at each tick pins it.
- **Five of my own tests had wrong arithmetic**, not wrong code (a block shorter than the pulse spacing, 40 BPM over one second of one-sample blocks giving 16
  pulses, a block start the run did not have, a wait too short for the first pulse, and a count that forgot pulses already stepped ahead). Each was fixed in the test's duration or offset and none by loosening what it asserts.

### Departures and defaults, flagged

Seven, all in **ADR-0009 amendment 1**: Start or Continue by position; pulses on the global grid; the first note 11 ticks after Start; the clock turned on mid-run says
where the sequencer is and turned off says nothing; `tick_position` is the audio's place; overflow is dropped and counted; Reset while running sends Stop.
One baseline entry is removed under ADR-0009 (`abi::the_module_reports_abi_version_one`, replaced by `..._two`).
