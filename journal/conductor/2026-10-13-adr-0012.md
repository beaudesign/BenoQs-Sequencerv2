## What I learned

- **`led` is a claim about the manual, not the plate.** The schema says `true` "when the manual gives the control an LED". The manual lights "the 1 position" (p54) and says the number field is a display; it does not say the dot beside each key is the key's own lamp. The repo already declined the same inference for Stop, Play and Pause, so the ten keys get no `led`, and the ADR says how it is added (an Amendments line, no layout change) if Q43 settles it. S1 does not wait: its asserted observables are on the meaning layer.
- The count assertions are eight lines in three files, not five, and two of them are in `apps/web/test/browser/`. The browser test's title contains "242"; no baseline line names a Node test (the baseline holds 499 Rust `test` ids and 58 `fixture` ids), so renaming the title takes nothing out of the ratchet.
- The step-2 pull request cannot be green at every commit: the layout test crosswalks `controls.json` and `panel.layout.json`. The ADR says so and offers the single-commit alternative, instead of claiming "no commit is red".
