# O6: what the link costs per Engine::render call. Measured, not asserted (a timing assertion on a shared runner would flake).
# release build, 100,000 renders of 256 samples at 48 kHz and 120 BPM, 10 tracks with 8 active steps each, 2 cores shared
# with a loom mutation run, so the medians carry about +-300 ns of noise (the two "no link" rows differ by that much).
# The engine was 47,552 bytes before and is 47,560 with the link field; the link itself is on the heap, made once by open_link.
no link                                      median    406 ns   p99   2209 ns
link, no commands (publish only)             median   1098 ns   p99   3303 ns
link, 1 command per render                   median   1125 ns   p99   3596 ns
link, 16 commands per render                 median   1516 ns   p99   4011 ns
link, 256 commands per render (the limit)    median   7797 ns   p99  34155 ns
no link (again)                              median    702 ns   p99   2963 ns
# Reading (first block, SetTrack only; see the second block for other command types): publishing the 8.4 KB snapshot costs about 0.7 us, and applying 256 SetTrack commands about 7 us, against a render budget of
# about 1.3 ms for "core apply, next tick" in docs/02 section 6. The max column (1 to 7 ms) is scheduler noise on a busy machine.

# Second block, added after the independent review of PR #11 found the first measured only the cheapest command. Source: handoffs/evidence/o6-cost/bench.rs
# (50,000 renders each, same machine and settings as above). The cap of 256 commands limits the count, not the cost.
no link                                              median    407 ns   p99   1442 ns   max  160429 ns
link, no commands (publish only)                     median   1105 ns   p99   2197 ns   max 1583176 ns
link, 1 SetTrack per render                          median   1192 ns   p99   3457 ns   max   78473 ns
link, 256 SetTrack per render (the limit)            median   7795 ns   p99  24942 ns   max 1714653 ns
link, 1 SetStep per render                           median   1121 ns   p99   2628 ns   max  523301 ns
link, 256 SetStep per render (the limit)             median   7876 ns   p99  25515 ns   max  564208 ns
link, 1 SetActivePage per render                     median   1117 ns   p99   2380 ns   max  172519 ns
link, 256 SetActivePage per render (the limit)       median   6963 ns   p99  27649 ns   max 2268972 ns
link, 1 Stop per render                              median   7084 ns   p99  19639 ns   max 1174384 ns
link, 256 Stop per render (the limit)                median  11769 ns   p99  29582 ns   max  812687 ns
link, 1 Reset per render                             median   6975 ns   p99  24302 ns   max  860076 ns
link, 256 Reset per render (the limit)               median  63674 ns   p99 201878 ns   max 2880283 ns
no link (again)                                      median    409 ns   p99   1465 ns   max  146024 ns
# Reading: 256 SetTrack, SetStep or SetActivePage commands cost about 7 to 8 us. 256 Stop commands about 12 us. 256 Reset commands about 64 us
# median and 202 us p99, which is still well under the 1.3 ms render budget in docs/02 section 6 but is 8 times the SetTrack figure.
