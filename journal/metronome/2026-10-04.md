### What happened

- The request file is carried here from #40 (identical, so the two merge cleanly) because this branch stands alone from `main`.
- **The acceptance test first** (`crates/octocore/tests/resume.rs`). Its oracle is one sentence: the stopped-and-played stream is the uninterrupted stream with the gaps cut out, plus, at each
  Stop, a Note Off for every note the receiver holds and CC 123 on those channels (and on all 32 after a Stop while stopped), minus the Note Off of a note the flush already ended. The sweep
  runs 60 patterns x 1 to 4 Stops x {the host's flag, commands} x {clock on, off} x 60/120/240 BPM x random buffers from 1 to 4096 samples. It is red on `main`: 60 of 60 scenarios differ.
- **The change** (`crates/octocore/src/engine.rs`, +158 -39). A Stop of a running transport keeps every queued event except the Note Off of a note whose Note On has gone out (a Note Off's
  Note On is the event made just before it, so the pairing is by creation number and exact when two notes of one pitch overlap), rebuilds the heap, and records where and when it stopped
  (`Resume`). Play moves the kept events, the tick clock and the tick ring by the gap, and steps on from the tick after the last one stepped. Nothing is rewound. `render` does not emit held
  events while stopped, `reset` drops them, `tick_position()` while stopped is where the audio stopped (continuous across the Play), and the clock sends the pulses of the held ticks at their
  moved samples after the `Continue`.
- **Tests that had to change, listed first as the commit says:** the golden `mcc_and_transport` (the only example that stops and plays; the log is the same events from the first Play on, moved
  to where the stream had them), and two in `clock.rs`: the first-pulse-after-a-Stop test now measures from the position the sound stopped at (name and claim kept), and the stopped
  `tick_position` test is renamed and says the new thing (one baseline removal, under ADR-0009 amendment 3).
- **Evidence:** `headless.py` section C 0 of 243 stop positions lose a note (was 100% / 25%); the shipped wasm 0 of 94 positions differ from the uninterrupted run with the gap taken out (was 92 of
  94); `cargo test --workspace` 465 passed; `npm test` 313, `npm run test:browser` 55. Mutation: 19 of 20 caught, 1 equivalent (`exact-resume-mutation-run.txt`).
- **ADR-0009 amendment 3** records the decision, the two things it amends in amendment 1 (where `Continue` falls, and `tick_position()` while stopped) and the price (an edit made while stopped
  to the step about to come is heard up to one step late, pinned by name in `resume.rs`). `tests/conformance/AMBIGUITIES.md` "lookahead, Stop and commands" is amended to match.

### What I learned and what surprised me

- **A step's note comes on the twelfth tick of its step, tick 11 and tick 23, not on tick 0 and tick 12.** I wrote the two hand-built tests on the wrong ticks and the first run said so; the engine's
  own pinned fact (`the_first_note_follows_start_by_the_eleven_ticks_the_engine_already_waits`) was in `clock.rs` all along.
- **The old engine's Play after a Stop could also play a note late.** It restarted the tick clock at the Play, so a note pulled early by a negative start offset was due before the first block and came
  out at sample 0 of it (`Diagnostics::late_events` was 1 in the first scenario of the sweep on `31d9c02`, which is how I noticed). A resume now carries on with ticks that were stepped 12 ticks ahead
  already, so the sweep asserts `late_events` is 0 after it. A Play from the very beginning (`Start`) is unchanged and can still do the same, because its first tick is at the Play; that is not part
  of this change.
- **Mutation found two weaknesses in my tests, as it usually does.** `tick_position` was checked a whole tick after the Play, by which time the held tick's own sample had taken over, so not moving the
  last sounded tick went unnoticed; and a Stop, Reset and Play left the held state ignored by every observable but the `Start` message. Both are fixed and rerun.
- **The pairing of Note Offs is the only subtle part.** By the first-on-first-off order of a channel's notes of one length the oracle in `resume.rs` can pair them, so the sweep uses one length
  per track and its own channel; the overlapping-pitch case with two lengths, where "the earliest Note Off" would be wrong, is built by hand in `two_notes_of_one_pitch_that_overlap_...`.

### Decisions taken on defaults

- **Pairing by creation number, not by a count per pitch.** A count per pitch drops "the earliest" Note Off, which is wrong exactly when an earlier note is longer than a later one of the same
  pitch (the hand-built test). The creation-number rule relies on `schedule_note` making the Note On and the Note Off one after the other, which it does and says.
- **`Start` for a resume only if nothing had sounded,** from the tick the stream carries on from, not from the engine's count. The two are the same in every state the engine can be in (R19 in the
  mutation record is an equivalent mutant); the code says what the message means.
- **The snapshot's `transport.tick` and `Engine::tick()` are the engine's own count, as before,** not the audio's position. Only `tick_position()` changed. If the page's playhead is drawn from the
  step positions in the snapshot, it shows the tracks' stepped-ahead position while stopped, up to a step ahead of where the sound resumes. That is the same as before and not part of this change;
  it is a Panelwright and Forge question when the playhead is made to follow the audio (noted in the PR).
- **The ABI is unchanged (version 2)** and `apps/web/engine/ABI.md` is not edited: its sentence about `octoweb_tick_position` ("it stands still while the transport is stopped") is still true.
- **`journal/STATE.md` and the P5 plan (#40) are not edited here,** because #38 and #40 edit the same lines; the Conductor updates both after they merge. The plan's W4 is met by this branch.
