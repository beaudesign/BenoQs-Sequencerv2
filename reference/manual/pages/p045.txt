Track position (POS)
The POS line will show the pattern of set steps in the track at hand.
Turning the POS knob will shift the steps around, depending on the turn direction, modifying the
contents of the play window, just to use the same terms.

Track direction (DIR)
This line indicates the chosen play direction for a track. Consider it as an index into the following
default mapping:
1 - Forward play
2 - Reverse play
3 - Ping-Pong
4 - Brownian, i.e. 2/3 probability forward, 1/3 probability reverse play
5 - Random order
6-16 - Same as 1, however: the track play directions 6-16 may also be individually edited and
changed as needed.
