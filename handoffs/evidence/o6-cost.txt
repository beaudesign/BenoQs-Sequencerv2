# O6: what the link costs per Engine::render call. Measured, not asserted (a timing assertion on a shared runner would flake).
# release build, 100,000 renders of 256 samples at 48 kHz and 120 BPM, 10 tracks with 8 active steps each, 2 cores shared
# with a loom mutation run, so the medians carry about +-300 ns of noise (the two "no link" rows differ by that much).
# The engine was 47,552 bytes before and is 47,560 with the link field; the link itself is on the heap, made once by open_link.
no link                                      median    406 ns   p99   2209 ns
link, no commands (publish only)             median   1098 ns   p99   3303 ns
link, 1 command per render                   median   1125 ns   p99   3596 ns
link, 16 commands per render                 median   1516 ns   p99   4011 ns
link, 256 commands per render (the limit)    median   7797 ns   p99  34155 ns
no link (again)                              median    702 ns   p99   2963 ns
# Reading: publishing the 8.4 KB snapshot costs about 0.7 us, and applying the full 256 commands about 7 us, against a render budget of
# about 1.3 ms for "core apply, next tick" in docs/02 section 6. The max column (1 to 7 ms) is scheduler noise on a busy machine.
