- The chords scene peaks at 410 to 430 events either way: with 512 it used 80% to 84% of the
  queue, with 1024 about 41%. That is the headroom this change buys.
- The dense scene still overflows at 1024 (187,409 refused in 10 s). It asks for more than any
  sensible queue holds. It is the overload scene, and the point is that the engine says so.
- The dense scene emitted 111,186 events at 512 and 197,282 at 1024, so the earlier
  "p50 8.9 us -> 2.8 us" comparison (two different scenes, one refusing two thirds of its
  notes) is not a like-for-like measurement and is withdrawn.
