## What I learned

- **The research changed the brief in five places** (README section 3): the rack is the 2026 one, not the 2018 setup;
  the Verbos module in it is the Harmonic Oscillator, not the Complex one SPEC-0004's S7 plans; the ER-101 has no
  64-step limit (the 64 is the Octopus's own chain, which the engine already has); the Optomix has no mode switch (I had
  assumed one in the research brief; the manual says the modes come from patching); and no schematic is public for the
  Make Noise, Verbos or Orthogonal modules, so "sound for sound" cannot be promised, only measured where documented and
  set by ear where not.
- Several manuals contradict themselves (which end of a knob is infinite decay, which way a depth control goes, a
  module's output range). They are listed in `modules.md` section 14, each with a named choice and a test, so a
  measurement can overturn it without surgery.
- **The owner's engineering spec arrived while I was working** (#71, opened 14:54 London, two minutes before the kit message). It reserves ADR-0013 for the shape player (so I renumbered the sound ADR to 0014 on #70, and #73 follows) and expects the shape player's spec to be `SPEC-0006` if free. It was not free: this is SPEC-0006, so the shape player's spec takes the next number. Its section 3.5 asks to be consulted before S0's `Voice` interface is touched, and K0 touches it (adds a bus voice); that consultation is written into README section 9.

## Review and corrections (r0.1, same day)

The owner asked for the work to be tested and evaluated. An independent reviewer fact-checked `modules.md` against the manuals
and read both files for consistency and soundness. I re-checked each serious finding against the source text before changing
anything. **Confirmed and corrected:**

- **Optomix Damp was backwards.** More Damp is a shorter, more muted decay (the manual compares it to muting a drum head). I had
  written the opposite in the sheet and in G-3.
- **Pluck Damp was backwards.** Damp right is the larger string with more harmonic content; left damps the most harmonics. I had
  written "a longer string is darker".
- **The clock claim.** The engine sends a MIDI clock only after `octoweb_set_clock(1)`, off after init, and turning it on puts the
  clock on the MIDI ports. The kit now takes its pulses from the tick position and run state the worklet already reads for the
  metronome. This also works where Web MIDI does not (iOS Safari). K-8 now asserts the clock master stays off.
- Smaller: the DPO timbre source (A's sine is the Mod Bus source, not the audio into the fold), Wogglebug Disturb (hold keeps the
  sample-and-hold value; it does not freeze every output), ER-101 duration zero (skipped; its clock cost is not stated), Pluck older
  notes (not stated), a "tighter than the maker" claim with no source (removed), Quad level's slice, the tier header, Clock and Out
  (now defined), eight source keys (now in the legend), K-5 and X-1 sizes (a sample in `just verify`, the full run separate), K-6
  (slowest block), K-12 versus K-10 (neutral keys), a loop-cut rule, the bus voice and H-16, and the dependency on SPEC-0004 S1, S2
  and S4.
- **One measurement.** A rack-shaped graph (four oscillator stacks, ladder, echo, drive, chorus, eight-line reverb) ran at about 13
  percent of real time in Node, and a generic 48-module walker added about 7 percent, under load from other builds. The 40 percent
  aim looks reachable on a desktop; a phone is the open risk. This is a feasibility figure, not the K-6 test.

**Not changed:** the reviewer's score was 8 of 10 on facts and 6 of 10 on consistency and on readiness; this commit addresses the
consistency items I could verify. Items left for the slice that builds them: numbers for T-3, E-4 and K-9.
