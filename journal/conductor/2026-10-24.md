## What I found

See `specs/SPEC-0008/README.md` section 3 (findings F-H1 to F-H12). The ones I would not have guessed before reading:

- Nothing persists. No `localStorage` or other storage in `apps/web/src` or `pages`, so a person with three synths picks every device again on every load.
- There is no way to say which track plays which channel from the page. The Track zoom `MCH` row is not built; `?route=` is the stand-in.
- `MidiScheduler.setOutput` swaps a device without sending the old one its owed Note Offs, and `send` has no `try`, so a device that throws would end
  the rest of that batch for every other device.
- A naive panic is wrong: Note Offs sent at once are overtaken by Note Ons already queued with a future timestamp, which is the hung note it was meant to cure.
- Continue goes out without a Song Position, so a drum machine that was stopped somewhere else resumes there.
- `ABLETON.md` section 8 still says the tick length is open and a step is "1.5 pulses". `abi.ts` says 2 ticks to the pulse, so a step is 6 pulses, a sixteenth.
  ADR-0009 amendment 1 item 3 quotes 28.6 ms for the first note after Start; at 48 ticks to the quarter it is 114.6 ms at 120 BPM. Both are stale, neither is fixed here.
