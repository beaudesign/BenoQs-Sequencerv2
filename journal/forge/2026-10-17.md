## What I learned

- `Plain` costs 12 to 15 percent of real time for 24 notes on an idle machine (five runs: 15.2, 14.3, 14.6, 12.4, 12.5). My first reading, 22 percent, was taken while other builds ran; the test measures this process's CPU time, which spares it from a busy neighbour but not from a shared core's cache and clock. So H-13's 25 percent stands unchanged and needs no amendment, but it leaves `Plain` a margin of two, not ten. The first heavier voice (S1) is the one that will find the real bound.
- A voice must not be able to say what it likes about velocity: H-9 and H-1 collide for `Plain` as written (r1.1 of the spec, ADR PR #70). The check runs both ways.
