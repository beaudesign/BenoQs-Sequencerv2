## What I learned

- The circle is not one thing to build. The numeric quadrant and the inner circle are, as far as the manual says, **LEDs on keys the plate already has** (p54, p57). So there is no display widget, no token and no look change to ask for. The work is a read model, a controller, and engine commands.
- **Number entry is stated once, on p17:** a double click sets the tens and a single click the ones; p52 and p57 say the circle's keys do the same. It is the rule behind tempo, velocity, certainty, program and bank.
- **The pitch ring shows the combined pitch of track and step** (p15). I had read it as the step's alone.
- **The 2007 tutorials are fixtures.** T01 to T06 are button-by-button, and the cheapest end-to-end check that the unique patterns are reachable from the panel alone.
- **The engine has most of the USP and no way to reach it.** Directions with `certainty_next`, chains, hypersteps, events, the effector, chords with polyphony, scales and multipliers all exist as state or logic. Only `SetTrack` (15 attributes) and `SetStep` (12) write musical state.
- About half the circle (multipliers, program and bank, map factors, the direction map, the grid scale) lives in Track and Grid modes, which are not built. The series is cut at a gate for that reason (D3).
