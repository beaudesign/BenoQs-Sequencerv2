### What I did

- `fire_step` now calls `resolve_phrase` when `Step::phrase` is `Some(1..=48)`.
  The step's own note always fires; enabled phrase notes enrich it as extras
  (PIT/VEL offsets, STA/LEN as extra 192-PPQN ticks). `PhraseNote::start_ticks`
  is `u8` because the factory charts go to 160.
- `Track::rotate_steps` / `rotate_skips` implement p.38-39. Wired through
  `queue_step_event` onto the event's own track (AMT is direction/distance).
  Hop-outs: skip, hyperstep, AMT=-127, and (chosen) VEL=-127 on a step event.
- Brownian statistical test: 400 seeds, asserts the 2/3 forward split.
- Fixture: `tests/conformance/phrases/forward_enriches_step.fixture`.
- Fixture DSL gained `phrase`/`skip`/`active` step attrs and `phrase N note I …`.

### What I learned

Inactive steps *do* participate in Track Rotate — p.38 says "all steps" except
skip/hyperstep/mask. First tests assumed only active steps rotated and failed
because step 15's empty content landed on step 0. The unused tail has to be
skipped in fixtures, not filtered in the algorithm.
