What you should notice is that the changes produced by the events will be bound between the base
value of the track for that attribute and the sum of the base and the interval size.

Event execution
Another detail that may be of interest is that events will always execute exactly on the beat and not
be influenced by the step's STA value to be pulled or pushed against the time line.

Step event offset reset
Finally, a step AMT of 0 (zero) will discard the offset that was produced by a step event.

AMT events
You may have noticed that while the AMT value of a step determines the effect an event has on its
respective attribute, the AMT map factor may be modified by events as well.
By creating AMT events, you are effectively able to have dynamic changes in the actual amount of
change that is being applied to an attribute map factor, so we will be seeing changes to the change
rate!
This is a powerful instrument to create evolving and to a large degree unpredictable sequences.
