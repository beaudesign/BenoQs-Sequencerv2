### What I learned and what surprised me

- **The old engine's note was due on tick 11 of a 12-tick step and so is the new one:** the first step of a Play sounds 11 ticks after it (62.5 ms at 120 BPM now). That is R0b and I parked it deliberately: it moves
  the goldens a second time, and the owner should be able to revert D0 without losing it or the reverse.
- **The lookahead scales with the tick.** `MAX_EARLY_TICKS` = 12 ticks was 31 ms and is now 125 ms, one step. Nothing breaks, but a command is heard up to a step later than before; it is in the plan as a stated consequence.
- **I lost 25 minutes waiting on a subagent that could have been doing a file of mine in parallel.** Next time I take my own files while it works.
- The owner's complaint was right on every count I measured: no chase-light, no sound, empty pattern, steps four times too fast, the first note late, two Play buttons. This is the first of those. R1 (it runs when opened)
  is next and is its own plan.
