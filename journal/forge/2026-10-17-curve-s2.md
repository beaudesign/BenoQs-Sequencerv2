## What I found

- **The page cannot enter a chord.** The ABI writes track attributes and the twelve step attributes, and the panel controller has no
  chord entry; a chord comes only from a loaded pattern. SPEC-0005 CV-11 says "for a chord track at strum 9". The page can make the
  other roll the style governs, a single note with a strum of 9 struck seven times (CV-6), through the same hook with `n = 7`, so the
  browser test and the ABI tests measure that roll against the table of section 4. The chord's roll is measured in `curve.rs` (S1). If the
  owner wants a chord on the page it needs a chord setter or the panel's chord entry, which is another request, not this slice.
- **The browser suite has two environment-sensitive tests.** With `CHROMIUM_PATH` pointing at the full Chrome binary, M8 (the browser's own
  MIDI refusal) and P11-1 (a 404 for a resource the headless shell does not ask for) fail on a branch that touches neither. They pass with
  Playwright's default headless shell. Not changed; the gate number above is the default.
- **A press is ten messages, and each posts a panel frame.** Harmless at a human rate. A single "all tracks" message would be tidier if
  a per-track picker never comes; D6 says it may.
- **The disk allowance ran out** in the middle of the build; I deleted stale build output (`target/` directories of finished worktrees
  and old reviewer builds in the scratchpad). No source or branch was touched.
