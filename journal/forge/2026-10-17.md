## What I learned

- The ADR is numbered **0014**, not 0013. The owner's engineering spec (#71, section 3.2, QG3) reserves 0013 for the shape player and says the sound ADR takes the next free number. This branch first named 0013 in comments and in this entry; they now say 0014 (the frozen copy of the monitor keeps "0013" in its header comment: the test pins that file byte for byte, and a comment is not worth a re-pin).

- The cost tests were not robust to a busy machine: the rig's "measures a real ratio" test failed at 29 percent while a cargo build ran next door, and the registry test read 20 to 21 percent with the suite running in parallel. The rig test now uses a bound of 1 (it is about the measurement) and the check takes the best of three runs; H-13's 25 percent is untouched, and the margin under parallel load is thin, which is the number S1 has to beat.
- `Plain` costs 12 to 15 percent of real time for 24 notes in a script outside the package tree (see the review follow-up: inside test/ the same check reads 20 to 22 percent) (five runs: 15.2, 14.3, 14.6, 12.4, 12.5). My first reading, 22 percent, was the one taken inside the test tree, and I put it down to other builds running; the review follow-up below shows that explanation was wrong. So H-13's 25 percent stands unchanged and needs no amendment, but it leaves `Plain` a margin of about three points, not ten. The first heavier voice (S1) is the one that will find the real bound.
- A voice must not be able to say what it likes about velocity: H-9 and H-1 collide for `Plain` as written (r1.1 of the spec, ADR PR #70). The check runs both ways.

## Review follow-up (WENGE-0032, after an independent review)

The owner asked for the work to be tested and evaluated. A separate reviewer fuzzed `Plain` against the frozen monitor (28.0 million
samples, three rates, block sizes 1 to 4096, bursts beyond the pool, velocity 0, unknown Note Offs, holds past ten seconds: no
difference) and then tried to build wrong voices that pass the rig. The core claim held; the rig was weaker than I had said.

**Fixed in this commit**

- **The rig ran at 48 kHz only** for pitch (H-2) and for the differential (H-1). A `Plain` with 48000 written into its phase step
  passed every test; the reviewer's fuzz caught it at 44.1 and 96 kHz. Now `checkPitch` also runs a spread of notes at 44.1 and 96 kHz,
  H-1 runs the demo and two storms at those rates, and a stub voice with its rate written in proves the pitch check can fail. Checked by
  hand: the same mutation of `plain.ts` now fails both.
- **A wrong explanation of the cost numbers.** I wrote that 12 to 15 percent was "idle" and 20 to 22 percent was "other builds running".
  The reviewer measured the same `checkCost(PLAIN)` at 12 to 13 percent from a script outside the package tree and 20 to 22 percent
  from a file inside `test/` (21.6 against 14.4 for one script moved between the two), with nothing else running. So the gap is not
  load. I do not know the cause. The 25 percent bound stands (it is the spec's), the margin is about three points, and S1 must find
  the cause or beat it.
- Wording: H-1 is block size 128 (the worklet's quantum), not "any block sizes"; the handoff cites SPEC-0004 r1.2 (in #70), where the
  approval record is.

**Not fixed in that commit: the rig's holes (closed in the next one, below).** Each passed the checks as written.

1. `start()` and `release()` are never timed: a `start()` of 4.9 ms (a block is 2.67 ms) passed at 7.8 percent.
2. Notes that end are counted as cheap: the same per-sample work reads 36 percent held and 4 percent if each note ends at 0.5 s;
   notes at or above MIDI 90 and velocities above 110 are never exercised; `checkCost` ignores `maxNotes`.
3. The allocation text check reads only `next(): number {...}`: it misses a helper method, `.push`, `Float64Array.from`,
   `Object.keys`, string building, `Array(8)`, an arrow-function `next` and an unannotated `next()`, and the directory scan is not recursive.
4. Nothing calls `start()` on a used note, yet the monitor reuses its 24 slots for ever: a voice that leaks filter state between notes passes.
5. Velocity brightness is measured at two velocities, 0.6 s in: a transient-only brightening and a non-monotonic dip both pass.
6. Determinism covers 20,000 samples at velocity 90: `Math.random` after 0.6 s passes.
7. Aliasing outside MIDI 60 to 84 and silence outside 24 to 96 pass (the spec allows both).

Also noted: a voice that decays within about two seconds while held cannot pass H-2, H-3 or H-9, which measure 0.6 to 2.3 s into a
held note (S4's gate pluck will meet this; the answer is a different measurement point, not a looser bound); the S0 row's "response"
measurement has no swept sine; the renderer has no npm script or `just` recipe and imports from `test/support`; a voice swap hard-cuts
sounding notes; `Monitor`'s voice setter accepts a voice that is not in the registry. The frozen file's header still says ADR-0013 and
"only the import path differs" (the class is renamed too); it cannot be edited without breaking its own pin.

### The rig holes, closed (S0 is approved; this is its rig, tests only, no `src/` change)

Red first: each hole has a stub voice that exploits it, and the rig test shows the check now fails it (and the old check let it by).

- **1 and 2, cost.** `checkCost` now runs two workloads and takes the worse: held (24 notes started once, as before) and churn (every
  note restarted every quarter second, staggered, across MIDI 24 to 108 and velocity 20 to 127). `start()` is paid for, a note that
  dies young cannot hide its sounding cost, and high notes and hard velocities are played. Stubs: `slowStart` (4 ms in `start()`) and
  `heavyDies` (heavy for 9000 samples then ends) pass the held workload and fail churn. `Plain` reads 20.8 percent (worst of the two).
- **3, allocation text.** The check now reads every method of every class except the constructor (`start`, `release`, getters, helpers,
  an arrow-function `next`, an unannotated `next()`), and flags `.push`, `Float64Array.from`, `Array(`, `Object.keys`, string building
  and `.toFixed` as well. Twelve forms are tested. A module-level function a method calls is still not looked at (stated in the doc
  comment). Mutation: an array literal added to `Plain.start()` now fails the registry test; it passed before.
- **5, velocity.** Five velocities, loudness monotone, centroid non-decreasing for a voice that declares yes, and for one that declares
  no the first 4096 samples are checked as well as the steady state. Stubs: an attack-only brightening and a rise that turns into a fall.
- **6, determinism.** Four notes of different pitch, velocity and length up to six seconds. Stub: `Math.random` once per 40,000 samples.
- **7, range.** A voice silent on a note in 24 to 96 is called silent (not "-100 cents"); the alias check also runs MIDI 36, 42, 48, 54, 90
  and 96 (the spec's range is 60 to 84; this only tightens). Stubs: silent above 90, a naive saw above 90.

**Left as a question for the Conductor, not built:** hole 4, a note's sound depending on what its slot played before. The monitor reuses
its 24 slots for ever, so a voice that leaks filter state between notes sounds different on a busy page than in the rig. SPEC-0004 does
not say whether per-slot state (a drifting detune, say) is allowed to carry across notes, and a check that forbids it could forbid S1's
drift (H-2). It is worth one sentence in the spec before S1.
