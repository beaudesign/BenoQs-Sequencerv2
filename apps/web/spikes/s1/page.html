Spike S1: how steady is Web MIDI output

WENGE P3 (SPEC-0002, WENGE-0014). This page plays every step of one track from the real engine, through the real worklet and scheduler, to one MIDI output, and listens on one MIDI input for them to come back. Connect the two with a loopback port (macOS: the IAC Driver, enabled in Audio MIDI Setup; Windows: loopMIDI). Use a loopback and not a synth: the run sends about 16 messages a second for as long as you choose.

Keep this tab in front for a first run. For a second run, start it and then switch to another tab for the whole time: the result records how long the tab was hidden and reports the two cases apart.
