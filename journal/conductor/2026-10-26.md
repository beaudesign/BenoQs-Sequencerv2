## What I found that changes the plan's shape

- **The Mother-32 follows MIDI Start and Stop by default, by user reports, and then ignores incoming notes while its sequencer runs.** Setup page 8, "Follow MIDI Start/Stop", turns it off (firmware v2.0).
  The spec's D-H7 sends Start and Stop to every chosen device, so with the default Play here starts its sequencer. That is Q-8-12 (default: notes only). It is the first place the owner's own gear
  contradicts a default I wrote.
- **No MIDI Out or Thru on the Mother-32 (the review), and no USB MIDI port found.** So reaching it from a browser needs something with a DIN output. Which is open (Q-8-1, still).
- **A polyphonic USB synthesiser and a one-voice DIN one** is criterion A6 and the best case for B3 (two paths, two latencies). A chord track routed to the Mother-32 plays one note, which one unknown.
- Two sources disagree on what the Mother-32 does with a Clock and no Start. I did not choose; B6(a) settles it.
