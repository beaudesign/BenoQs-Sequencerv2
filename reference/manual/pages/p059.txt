The Effector

One mutator button was not mentioned yet, and that is the EFF mutator. What is EFF?
To be precise, EFF is not as much a function as it is a state switch,
enabling the track to participate in cross modulation across the PAGE. Some additional explanation
is needed here, so let's start with the theory.
In each Octopus page there is the notion of an Effector. In simple terms, the effector is a mechanism
allowing tracks inside a page to modulate other tracks inside the same page.
Modulation here refers to projecting Step attribute offsets from one track to another at a time at
which the modulator track is being played.
The affected Step attributes are Step VEL, PIT, LEN and MCC.
