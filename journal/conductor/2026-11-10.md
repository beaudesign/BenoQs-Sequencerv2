## What I learned

- **The Figma file's quiet inks fail the floor.** `#252525` at 60% is 4.1:1 on the paper and at 35% it is 2.1:1. The nearest tone that keeps 7:1 on the darkest ground it meets (the well) is `#4d4d4c`. This
  is Q-9-1, and it is the owner's to settle; lowering the floor would be a loosened threshold.
- **The paper is inside S8's cluster**, about ΔE 1.8 (CIE76) from `#F4F1EA`. S8 has the exit: a suppression that says why. The reason is the owner's own Figma variable.
- **The plate and the paper are nearly one tone** (1.007:1). The dark case is the only edge between the instrument and the page. The well under it helps a little (1.07:1 against the plate). Q-9-2.
- **16 and 8 are one decision, not two.** A control that sits 8 from a 16 px corner has a concentric arc at 8, which is the corner the strip already has. So the contract gains one key and loses none.
- **A sheet that docks needs no shadow.** The references float their panels and shade them; `docs/05` section 1 forbids covering the panel, and once the sheet sits beside the stage S4 costs nothing.
- **A local file with no viewport tag renders at 980 px wide** in the phone check. The prototype now carries the tag; the first phone check told me nothing.
