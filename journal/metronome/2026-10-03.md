### What I learned and what surprised me

- **My first draft of the ties test did not pin the rule.** A quantiser that never looks up passed it and the chord tests, because every pitch they ask about has a scale tone below it at
  least as near as the one above. A tone that is nearer above (F# in C D E G A) is the only thing that tells it apart: S2, and NEARER.
- **The effector sum and the scale are ordered in one line of `engine.rs`** (`final_pit` is summed, then forced). ENGINE is the only test that notices if they swap, and the swap is easy to
  make by accident: the pure tests do not see it.
- I said in the description of PR #34 that the diatonic result was "the manual's claim about chord building". It is not: it is a property of the engine's tie rule, which the manual does not
  state. The note itself says so (section 2.3); the PR text was wrong, and the test file's header says it again.
